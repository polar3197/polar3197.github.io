---

I miss training models and wrangling data. So I contrived this project because it will be mildly useful in my day to day travel around sf.

Some goalposts:
- advise you on when low-occupancy buses are coming to your commute stop
- infer where/when most riders get on buses
- try to beat 511's predictions api with small decision tree models
