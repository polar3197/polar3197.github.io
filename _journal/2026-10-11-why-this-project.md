---
project: buni
title: "why this project?"
---

sometimes i see a bus coming and wonder where it's going.

i wanted a quick way of punching in a route and seeing its path. 

as i built this i realized you could check occupancies and arrival times.

what would make the ultimate sf public transit app?

cool things within reach:

- advise you on when low-occupancy buses are coming to your commute stop
- infer where most riders get on buses at certain times of day
- try to beat 511's predictions api with small decision tree models
