## Context

The map is the entrypoint to all of this web-app's functionality. 

I am enamored by the ability to see all of the buses all at once. It is refreshing to see the big picture and to only filter down to individual needs when necessary.

I want each vehicle icon to be clickable, upon which, details will display,
* vehicle_id
* speed
* direction
* next_stop
* stated arrival time at next stop
* predicted arrival time at next stop

I want each route in the sidebar to be clickable, upon which it will expand and display
* route_id
* number of buses active
* hours of operation
* ...

I will also find room for two more form fields
1. origin & destination (as either lat,lon or as stop names/IDs) --> optimal route
2. text field for semantic querying of the database and recieving LLM generated response
